Campus outfits styled across seasons, comfort, and everyday expression

Welcome to my college wardrobe diary. I saved a photo of every outfit I wore to college.You can use it for fashion ideas, and I’ll keep it to look back on my college days.

This archive covers all 4 years of my college life—organized by semester and season. Even semesters (summer/spring outfits) and odd semesters (fall/winter outfits) are sorted separately so you can easily find what works for that time of year. Beyond daily looks, there's also a special section for occasion outfits—like Saraswati Puja, orientation day, club welcome programs, and other college events. Whether you're looking for everyday campus inspiration or ideas for special college moments, you'll find it here. For me, it's a way to preserve these memories; for you, it's a real, relatable style guide.

Right now, I've uploaded outfits up to my 5th semester, and I'll keep adding more as I go through each new semester.
